note i16lk look sync try 3 (w/i16lk, flags natlooksec 1, natmutlook 1, natgenmk 1). Check 1 counters: B starts with natgenmk 0, so the copies spawn without the attaches the client generator skipped (humanos_com_diferenca / convergindo > 0); after 25 s natgenmk 1 and the next apply creates them: partes_do_host>0 feitas>0 falhou=0 refeitas=0 in one window. Then the copies settle: com_diferenca=0 diferencas=0 humanos_com_diferenca=0. Then natlookre 1 (dev flag, default 0) makes every live client copy run apply_look once more while it already has its made parts: that window shows partes_do_host>0 feitas=0 refeitas=0 (partes_do_host counts host parts covered by a made part or still to make; refeitas counts a make for an actor+key made before; the old DLL dropped every made part in gen_apply before checking the generator picks and re-made them on each apply, so feitas>0 refeitas>0), the copies settle again and no later window shows feitas>0 or refeitas>0. convergindo= counts copies younger than 30 s; every copy older than 30 s with a difference counts in com_diferenca / humanos_com_diferenca. Old DLL: no refeitas= field, or feitas>0 in later windows.
